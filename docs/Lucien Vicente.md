**Toreador elder, Camarilla. PARIS’s former Auspex teacher, mentor, and lover.**

#### **Core Concept**

An old Toreador who followed PARIS across an ocean and then chose the Camarilla anyway. He’s been sent to watch the person he loved and bring them in if they go too far, and he decides what counts as too far. He thinks it’s his chance at redemption after decades. It’s also a test, and he doesn’t know it.

#### **Names**

* **Lucien Vicente:** his name in life.  
* **“Vincent”:** the party’s working name, taken from the moment he flinched at “Dinner Party.” The song’s “Vincent” is a mask of “Vicente.”  
* If he’s the song’s Vincent (“sulking since the Armada,” 1588), he’s at least \~409 in 1997\.

#### **History**

* **Versailles** (a deliberate anachronism): Lucien was a Toreador Primogen to the Prince of Versailles. Yun Jeonghee, a Ventrue, was sent as a diplomatic ambassador. The two clans weren’t at war, but before the Axiom alliance they weren’t friendly either.  
* Lucien was immediately enthralled. Yun, more aloof, eventually fell in love too. They kept it secret because it created conflicts of interest for both.  
* Yun grew fed up with the waiting and the impotence of vampire leadership and planned to leave for America.  
* **Marguerite**, a Ventrue primogen from Paris, discovered the affair and threatened to expose them to the primogeniture. That forced Yun’s hand, and Lucien went along to avoid disgrace.  
* **The exodus (\~1939-41, early WWII, before America entered):** Lucien taught Yun Auspex on the way. They didn’t get past the first levels.  
* **In America:** Yun wanted out of the Camarilla. Lucien felt bound to tradition and swore fealty to the Prince of San Francisco. They parted.  
* Lucien has always watched Yun. When Yun emerged as the musician PARIS, watching became his assignment.

#### **Relationship to PARIS**

* **Mentor and lover**, a Romeo and Juliet affair across clans, for many years.  
* Yun was trained to enter any room with Presence on. It took decades to train that out. Presence worked on Lucien, but Yun never meant to lure him.  
* PARIS recognized him on sight at Roderick’s and played to him anyway. PARIS’s method is drawing out individual vampires, so the flinch is that working.  
* PARIS has people (ghouls and recruited vampire ex-pats) watching and following Lucien. Lucien doesn’t know he’s PARIS’s source, though he probably suspects.

#### **Orders and the Leash**

* **Standing orders:** watch PARIS, report, and bring them in if they expose too much. Lucien decides what counts as too much. He reports factually, and PARIS used no powers at Roderick’s.  
* **Who knows he helped PARIS leave the Ventrue:** the Toreador prince and Lucien’s Toreador elders. The court as an institution doesn’t. Marguerite knows about the affair and never told, and her silence was leverage that weakened once they left Versailles. The prince likely knows they were lovers, and Lucien knows the prince knows.  
* **The prince doesn’t trust him, and Lucien doesn’t know.** The prince suspects Lucien is the leak.  
* **The Sheriff** is a Toreador and the prince’s trusted ally, and watches Lucien. If they think Lucien isn’t acting when he should, they bring both Lucien and PARIS to the prince. They don’t know about the exit or the affair, weren’t at Roderick’s, and will be at the Paramount, where they plan to observe and wait. Lucien will probably notice them and realize he’s in danger. There is no canon Sheriff, and this one is unbuilt.  
* **The Ventrue elders** want PARIS back and assume this is a rebellious phase. They haven’t chosen force.

#### **At Roderick’s**

* He flinched at “Dinner Party,” stayed until the show ended, and left the venue then. Roderick’s stays open as a goth club, so he didn’t leave early.  
* Luther saw him outside on a phone, and the next moment he was gone. No one saw how, and there was no breach.  
* His call went to the prince’s staff, since any Axiom contact flows through the nobility. He was gone before the outies met PARIS backstage, and he didn’t notice Luther.

#### **What He Knows**

* He doesn’t know the outies met PARIS.  
* He doesn’t know the prince distrusts him.
